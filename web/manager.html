A new assistant

An empty folder becomes its home: its memory, its Spark, its pictures and its backups, kept apart from the others. It thinks through the same model as the first assistant here until you change that in its own Settings.

Before talking to it much, write its Spark: data\spark.md in its folder, the text it reads as itself.

Attach an assistant

An assistant that already has a home -- one that was detached and moved, or made somewhere else. Choose the folder that holds its identity.json; it is started and listed again, with its memory exactly as it left it.
